Fredrik chats with [James Smith](https://floppy.org.uk/) about forking Rails for the greater good. The name of the project is [Mosscap](https://mosscap.dev/). During recording, it still used the temporary name Amiko.

The importance of getting more people involved, quickly.

A place to keep maintaining existing Rails apps, but not necessarily a place to start completely new apps. There may not even be a second major version of Mosscap. There are other projects for that situation.

We also discuss James' project Manyfold and how it works to apply for and build software funded by NLNet.
