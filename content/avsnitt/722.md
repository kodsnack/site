Fredrik talks to [Koushik Dasika](https://www.linkedin.com/in/koushikdasika/) about working with [Elixir](https://elixir-lang.org/). From discovery to first deployments. Via appreciation of a big mess that gets the job done just keeps on working. And on to teaching Elixir to others.

Since we met at [Goatmire](https://goatmire.com/) last year, and Goatmire is running when this episode is released, we of course talk about some conference highlights, and about meetups and conferences able to ignite that exciting feeling that there is a lot to discover and learn in the world.

Oh, and what *was* that thing about seals?
