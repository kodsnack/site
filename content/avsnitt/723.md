Fredrik talks to [Sam Aaron](https://linktr.ee/samaaron) about [Sonic pi](https://sonic-pi.net/index.html) - the tool for learning, coding, and performing music. Whe discuss where it stated but more importantly how it has evolved during the past year - coming to the web and gaining lots of performance and features in the process.

Sam also discusses how to build reliable things when working on an indisde-out bottle ship, blindfolded and with a gaslighting agent as your help.

Recorded during the excellent [Goatmire Elixir 2026](https://2026.goatmire.com/) conference.
